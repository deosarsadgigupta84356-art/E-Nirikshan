THE FIELD JOURNEY

From package
to evidence.

The interface follows the actual evidence chain instead of pretending that an AI score is the final answer. Offline capture is bounded; heavier processing waits for sync.

REAL MARKET PHOTOGRAPH
01 · TRACEABLE CAPTURE

Record the scene before interpreting it.

Every inspector photograph is associated with case identity, inspector identity, evidence sequence, GPS coordinates and accuracy, capture or sync time, and a SHA-256 fingerprint.

The interface deliberately separates what the camera captured from what later extractors or rules may infer. A photograph can establish traceability without, by itself, proving the authenticity of GPS or the scene.

SHA-256 is a cryptographic fingerprint of the image bytes. If the file changes, its SHA-256 value changes. It helps prove that the reviewed file is the same file that was captured or stored; it does not by itself prove that the scene or GPS claim is authentic.
ABOUT THE CONCEPT

Built around
evidence, not theatre.

The interface takes its terminology and workflow from the supplied E-Niriksha requirements, while the visual language is deliberately grounded in Indian field context.

What belongs here

Package-adaptive capture, bounded offline work, evidence provenance, six declaration groups, Visual Compliance Map states, role-based workflows, officer verification and citizen preliminary reporting.

  • Commodity identity
  • Manufacturer / packer
  • Net quantity
  • MRP / retail sale price
  • Month / year manufacture
  • Consumer care

Prototype scope

This prototype demonstrates the evidence-first workflow in a browser. The screen states mirror the supplied workflow: field capture, provenance, evidence review, officer verification and reporting.

Officer verification: AI-supported evidence is shown separately from the officer decision. The responsible officer remains the final verifier.
